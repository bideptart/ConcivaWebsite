FAQ

Questions leaders ask before rollout.

Can Conciva support both service and revenue teams?

Yes. Conciva is designed for shared ownership across support, SDR, account management, and customer success teams. Each team can keep tailored views while operating from the same conversation layer.

How much control do we keep over AI actions?

You define approval thresholds, automation triggers, knowledge sources, and escalation rules. Teams can start with assisted drafting and gradually unlock more automation where confidence is high.

Does Conciva replace our CRM and help desk?

No. Conciva acts as the operating layer above the systems you already use. It unifies context, coordination, and AI execution while syncing with your existing CRM and support tools.

What does onboarding usually look like?

Most teams begin with one shared queue, a small set of automations, and role-based AI copilots. From there, Conciva expands into call prep, playbooks, and leadership analytics as the operating model matures.
